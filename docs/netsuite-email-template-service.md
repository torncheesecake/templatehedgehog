# NetSuite-Compatible Email Template Service Notes

## Public Positioning

- Offer: production-ready HTML email templates for NetSuite marketing campaigns.
- Claim boundary: HTML email template production only, not NetSuite consulting, implementation, automation, or account administration.
- Product connection: the service is powered by the same production email system behind Template Hedgehog.

## Revenue Maths

- 4 Email System projects per month at £3,000+ creates £12,000+ monthly service revenue.
- 7 Campaign Kit projects per month at £1,500 creates £10,500 monthly service revenue.
- A mixed month can reach £10,000 faster than passive product-only sales, for example 2 Email System projects, 2 Campaign Kits, and recurring support retainers.

## Commercial Logic

Service-led work creates a higher-ticket path while Template Hedgehog remains the proof, tooling, and production system. The public page should sell the pain of dated templates, broken layouts, poor mobile rendering, slow campaign production, and fragile raw HTML without implying NetSuite implementation expertise.
