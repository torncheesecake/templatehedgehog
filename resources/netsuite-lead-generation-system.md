# NetSuite Email Template Service Lead Generation System

## Goal

Turn NetSuite-compatible email template enquiries into paid fixed-price work without claiming NetSuite consulting, implementation, scripting, CRM migration, or campaign strategy expertise.

## Core Offer

Production-ready HTML email templates for NetSuite marketing campaigns.

The service sells a narrow outcome:

- cleaner campaign email HTML
- responsive layouts
- editable copy and image areas
- reusable sections where useful
- handoff notes for teams uploading and configuring campaigns in NetSuite

## Lead Capture Paths

### Primary Path: Template Review

Use when a prospect has an existing template, a broken email, or a live campaign need.

Ask for:

- current HTML or MJML
- screenshots of desktop and mobile rendering
- campaign type
- brand guidelines or site URL
- target audience
- example emails they like
- preferred package if known
- timeline
- approval process

Outcome:

- confirm fit or no fit
- recommend Starter Template, Campaign Kit, or Email System
- identify missing assets
- move to proposal

### Secondary Path: Fit Question

Use when the prospect is unsure whether the problem is template production or wider NetSuite work.

Ask for:

- how they use NetSuite marketing campaigns
- what currently breaks or slows them down
- whether they need template HTML, upload help, campaign setup, scripting, or strategy
- timeline and budget range if available

Outcome:

- accept if it is template production
- refer or decline if it is implementation, scripting, migration, or strategy only

## Qualification Rules

Qualified:

- uses NetSuite marketing campaigns or raw campaign HTML
- has a template quality, mobile rendering, editing, or repeatability problem
- can provide current HTML, screenshots, or brand assets
- accepts fixed-price template production scope

Needs Caution:

- wants campaign strategy but no template build
- cannot provide assets or approval owner
- needs urgent turnaround without clear content
- expects upload and campaign configuration without a separate agreement

Not Qualified:

- full NetSuite implementation
- CRM migration
- custom NetSuite scripting
- sales or marketing automation architecture
- Oracle NetSuite support issues

## Contact Workflow

1. Capture enquiry through template review or fit question.
2. Log the lead in the outreach tracker.
3. Reply with acknowledgement and missing information checklist.
4. Review template, screenshots, campaign type, and assets.
5. Decide fit, package, risks, and timeline.
6. Send proposal with scope, exclusions, price, payment terms, and next step.
7. On acceptance, send project kickoff and asset request.
8. Build, QA, revise, and hand off.
9. Request testimonial and proof assets after successful delivery.
10. Add approved proof to case-study pipeline.

## Revenue Operating Rhythm

Weekly:

- send targeted outreach to NetSuite campaign users
- follow up open leads
- review tracker stages
- move stalled leads to nurture or close-lost
- update case-study proof targets from delivered work

Monthly:

- review package conversion by source
- record revenue from Starter, Campaign Kit, Email System, and support
- identify one proof asset to publish or prepare
- tune outreach copy using real objections from calls and emails
