# NetSuite Email Template Service Case-Study Collection Flow

## Purpose

Collect credible proof from early projects without inventing results or overstating NetSuite expertise.

The case study should show the template production problem, the work delivered, and the practical outcome.

## Proof to Collect During the Project

- current template HTML or screenshot
- broken mobile screenshot if available
- notes on what made editing difficult
- new template screenshot
- list of reusable sections delivered
- handoff notes
- client feedback
- time saved or reduced friction if the client can estimate it

## Case-Study Structure

### 1. Situation

What the client had before:

- dated email template
- fragile HTML
- poor mobile rendering
- inconsistent campaign layouts
- slow editing process

### 2. Scope

What was agreed:

- package
- number of templates
- source and compiled files
- reusable sections
- handoff notes
- clear exclusions

### 3. Delivery

What was built:

- responsive HTML
- editable areas
- shared header/footer
- reusable sections
- QA checks
- handoff guide

### 4. Outcome

What changed:

- easier editing
- cleaner mobile layout
- more consistent campaign templates
- faster campaign preparation
- fewer layout defects after edits

Only use outcomes the client confirms.

### 5. Permission

Record:

- public, anonymised, or private proof only
- approved screenshots
- approved quotes
- approved company name or anonymous descriptor
- usage restrictions

## Client Permission Email

```text
Hi {{first_name}},

I am collecting practical proof from the NetSuite-compatible email template service.

Would you be comfortable with me turning this project into a short case-study note?

It can be anonymised. I would only include approved details, such as the starting problem, what was delivered, before/after screenshots if allowed, and the practical result for your team.

Nothing will be published without your approval.

Matthew
```
