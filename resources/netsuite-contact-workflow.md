# NetSuite Email Template Service Contact Workflow

## Stage 1: New Enquiry

Trigger:

- prospect books a template review
- prospect sends a current template
- prospect replies to outreach
- prospect asks whether the service is a fit

First response goal:

- acknowledge the enquiry
- confirm this is template production, not NetSuite implementation
- ask for any missing brief items
- set the next step as a short recommendation or proposal

Suggested reply:

```text
Hi {{first_name}},

Thanks for sending this over.

I can help if the issue is campaign email HTML quality, mobile rendering, editing fragility, or reusable NetSuite campaign templates.

To scope it properly, could you send:

- current template HTML or MJML
- screenshots of the current email
- campaign type
- brand guidelines or site URL
- target audience
- examples of emails you like
- ideal timing

Once I have that, I can recommend whether this is a Starter Template, Campaign Kit, Email System, or not a fit.

Matthew
```

## Stage 2: Qualification

Check:

- Do they use NetSuite marketing campaigns?
- Is the problem template production?
- Do they have current HTML, screenshots, brand assets, or example emails?
- Is there a clear campaign type?
- Is there a realistic timeline?
- Who approves the work?
- Do they understand upload and campaign configuration are their responsibility unless separately agreed?

Decision:

- qualified, send proposal
- needs more information
- no fit, politely decline or refer

## Stage 3: Proposal

Send a proposal when:

- the template problem is clear
- the package fit is clear
- assets are available or gaps are known
- approval owner is known
- exclusions are clear

Proposal must include:

- package
- price
- deliverables
- assumptions
- exclusions
- revision process
- payment terms
- start requirements
- next step

## Stage 4: Kickoff

After acceptance:

- confirm package and scope
- request final assets
- confirm dates
- confirm feedback owner
- confirm handoff format
- confirm customer owns upload and campaign configuration unless separately agreed

## Stage 5: Delivery

During build:

- produce HTML and source files as agreed
- keep editable areas obvious
- QA structure and rendering
- send preview or screenshots for review
- collect one consolidated revision round where included

## Stage 6: Handoff

Send:

- final compiled HTML
- source MJML if included
- image requirements or hosted URL notes
- editable-area notes
- upload and configuration boundary reminder
- support or revision window

## Stage 7: Proof Collection

After approval:

- ask for a short testimonial
- request permission to use anonymised before/after proof
- record measurable outcomes where possible
- move the project into the case-study collection flow
