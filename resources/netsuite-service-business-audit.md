# NetSuite Template Service Business Audit

## Offer Assessment

The service can stand alone if it is sold as a narrow production offer:

- production-ready HTML email templates for NetSuite marketing campaigns
- fixed-price delivery
- clear template handoff
- no claim to NetSuite implementation, CRM migration, scripting, or campaign strategy

The strongest commercial angle is not general NetSuite expertise. It is removing fragile campaign HTML from teams that already use NetSuite campaigns.

## Conversion Improvements

- Lead with the specific buyer: NetSuite campaign teams and marketers editing raw HTML.
- Make the first action a template review rather than a generic contact.
- Show package fit so buyers can self-select before emailing.
- Explain the enquiry flow from current template to fixed-price scope.
- Keep the not-fit criteria visible to reduce bad enquiries.

## Credibility Improvements

- State practical NetSuite campaign template experience without overstating implementation expertise.
- Connect the service to Template Hedgehog as production tooling and proof.
- Use case-study placeholders as future proof targets, not as fake claims.
- Collect before/after screenshots, mobile rendering notes, time saved, and reusable-section counts from early projects.

## Lead Capture Improvements

- Ask for current HTML, screenshots, campaign type, brand guidelines, target audience, and example emails.
- Offer two enquiry paths: template review and fit question.
- Keep the secondary path for people who are unsure whether the work is in scope.

## Case Study Pipeline

- Template refresh: one broken or dated template rebuilt into cleaner responsive HTML.
- Campaign kit rebuild: three mismatched campaign templates turned into a consistent set.
- Reusable email system: multiple layouts and componentised sections for repeat campaign production.

Each first project should be delivered with permission to later use anonymised before/after proof if the customer agrees.
